This Is DJ
Allmighty Mix

Born and raised in Haiti, now residing in New York, DJ Allmightymix brings a unique flare to events, productions, and parties across a multitude of cultural and demographic platforms.

Although notorious for mixing R&B and Hip Hop, DJ Allmightymix has been known to mix all genres of music, including Konpa, Latin, Afro, Dancehall, Reggae, House and more.
