This Is DJ
Allmighty Mix

Born and raised in Haiti, now residing in New York, DJ Allmightymix brings a unique flare to events, productions, and parties across a multitude of cultural and demographic platforms.

Although notorious for mixing R&B and Hip Hop, DJ Allmightymix has been known to mix all genres of music, including Konpa, Latin, Afro, Dancehall, Reggae, House and more.

This genre-spanning skill set makes him a fit for a wide range of events — weddings and cultural celebrations that call for Konpa and Latin flavor, club nights built on Hip Hop and House, and multicultural gatherings that need a DJ fluent in more than one sound. Across every genre, the throughline stays the same: a smooth, high-energy mix that keeps the dance floor moving from open to close.
